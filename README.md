# SkyDash
SkyDash is a selfhosted app for Wx Station management and has support for Satellites such as GOES as well!
More is to come in the future, the base of it is being made now.
What it has now (in dev stages, most has been made and will be published soon): GOES satellite support, Wx Custom station support (i.e. AWN, AcuRite, Ecowitt, and more),
Wind Rose for 24hr {AWN fashon}, GOV stations (currently US support/NOAA), custom sensors support (AWN/Ecowitt temp and lightning sensors) with more to come, Webhook support, custom Satellite support (using Goestools), and many more!
